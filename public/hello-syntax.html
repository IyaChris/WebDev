Welcome to my weather website! Here, you can check the current weather in University Park on the Weather page, enjoy curated rain videos designed for relaxation and focus, and explore a rain-themed audio playlist with hours of calming soundscapes on the Sounds page. This project grew from my love of rain, both as a source of comfort and as a tool for studying and sleep. As my first full HTML project, it allowed me to build new skills, experiment with design, and integrate an API for real-time data. I am excited to continue expanding this project and applying what I have learned to future web development work!
